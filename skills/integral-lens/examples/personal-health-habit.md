# Example — Grow → Plan (Quick depth)

**User prompt:**
> I've tried to start exercising five times this year and quit every time after two
> weeks. Quick integral take?

---

## Integral Lens: restarting exercise (quick)
**Mode:** Grow → Plan · **Depth:** Quick

**I — UL:** Exercise is framed as a punishment for "being lazy"; motivation depends on
guilt, which runs out in about two weeks.
**It — UR:** Plans start too big (5×/week, an hour each); evenings are when energy is
lowest.
**We — LL:** No one in your life exercises with you or asks about it; at home it is a
private struggle, not a shared value.
**Its — LR:** The gym is 25 minutes away; your schedule has no protected slot.

**Biggest blind spot:** you have been treating it as a willpower (UL) problem, while
the strongest forces against you are environment and social support (LR, LL).

**Partial truth:** you're right that motivation matters — but meaning, not guilt,
is the fuel that lasts.

### Start in the next 48 hours
- [ ] **(UL · Clean Up)** Write one sentence on *why* you want to move that isn't about
  guilt — e.g. "to have energy for my kids". Put it on your phone's lock screen.
- [ ] **(LL · Show Up)** Ask one friend to walk with you twice a week, same time.
- [ ] **(LR · Show Up)** Pick a 20-minute home or neighborhood routine, scheduled in the
  morning, with shoes left by the door.

**Metrics:** I — energy 1–10 each evening · It — sessions per week · We — walks with
your friend · Its — streak on the calendar.
