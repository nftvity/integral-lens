# Example — Diagnose → Plan (Standard depth)

**User prompt:**
> My 12-person startup keeps missing sprint deadlines. We've tried Jira, a new PM, and
> daily standups. Nothing works. What are we missing?

---

## Integral Lens: a 12-person startup that keeps missing deadlines
**Mode:** Diagnose → Plan · **Depth:** Standard · **Vantage point:** the founder

**Assumptions:** seed-stage, founder-led, engineers mostly remote, no clear product owner
besides the founder.

### The Integral Picture
Every fix so far has been in the exterior quadrants — tools (Jira), roles (a new PM),
and rituals (standups). Those are good, but deadlines are slipping for reasons that
live mainly in the interior quadrants: priorities change whenever the founder has a new
insight, engineers do not feel safe saying "this estimate is unrealistic", and nobody
shares a clear picture of what "done" means. The system is running an Orange
achievement process on top of a culture that quietly defers to the founder (an Amber
pattern), so the process is followed in form but not in spirit.

### Four Quadrants
| | Interior | Exterior |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | **I — UL**<br>• Founder: high urgency, fear of losing the market window, attachment to new ideas<br>• Engineers: low ownership of estimates, quiet frustration at changing priorities | **It — UR**<br>• Work-in-progress is high; tasks are started but not finished<br>• Estimates are made in minutes, rarely revisited<br>• Founder messages engineers directly with new requests |
| **Collective** | **We — LL**<br>• "The founder decides" norm; disagreement feels risky<br>• No shared definition of done<br>• Missing deadlines has become normal, even joked about | **Its — LR**<br>• Jira, new PM and standups in place<br>• No WIP limits; no protected sprint scope<br>• Two decision channels: the PM's backlog and the founder's DMs |

**Overweighted:** LR (tools, process). **Neglected:** LL (safety, shared norms) and the
founder's UL.

### Levels · Lines · States · Types
- **Levels:** team culture appears to sit around Amber/Orange: an Orange process
  (sprints, metrics) with an Amber authority pattern (the founder is the final word).
  The founder likely operates from an Orange Achiever logic — strong on goals, less on
  the systemic effects of their own behavior.
- **Lines:** founder strong in cognitive and values (vision), weaker in interpersonal
  (noticing silence). Team strong in technical skill, weaker in voicing disagreement.
- **States:** chronic urgency and low-grade stress; decisions taken in "fire mode".
- **Types:** a high-agency, intuitive founder with a more reflective, detail-oriented
  team — a style clash that reads as "they're slow" / "he's chaotic".

### Blind Spots & Partial Truths
- **Blind spot:** the founder's own behavior is the main source of scope change.
- **The founder is right** that speed and adaptation matter at this stage.
- **The engineers are right** that constant change makes any deadline fiction.
- **The PM is right** that the process could work if it were protected.

### Leverage Points
1. **One channel for new work** (LR) **agreed in a real conversation** (LL) — it moves
   founder behavior (UR) and team trust (LL) at once.
2. **A shared, written definition of done** — it aligns meaning (LL) and behavior (UR).

### Integral Action Plan
| # | Task | Quadrant | Up | Owner | Effort | Impact | Done when |
|---|------|----------|----|-------|--------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Founder reflects: "What am I afraid will happen if I don't change priorities?" (15-min journal, then share with a coach/peer) | UL | Clean Up | Founder | S | High | Fear named and shared with one person |
| 2 | Blameless 60-min retro: "Why do sprints slip?" with an explicit rule that the founder speaks last | LL | Clean Up | PM | S | High | 3 agreed changes written down |
| 3 | Write a one-page definition of done together | LL | Show Up | Team | S | High | Pinned in the team channel |
| 4 | All new requests go through the PM's intake; founder stops DMing engineers with work | LR | Show Up | Founder + PM | S | High | Zero work DMs for 2 sprints |
| 5 | Set a WIP limit of 2 tasks per engineer | LR | Show Up | PM | S | Med | Board enforces the limit |
| 6 | Engineers estimate in pairs and flag any estimate they don't believe | UR | Grow Up | Engineers | S | Med | Each sprint has ≥1 flagged estimate discussed |
| 7 | Mid-sprint "change budget": one swap allowed, anything else waits | LR | Show Up | PM | S | High | Rule followed for 3 sprints |
| 8 | Monthly founder–team "strategy hour" where new ideas are shared and ranked | LL | Grow Up | Founder | M | Med | Ideas land in the backlog, not in sprints |

**Start in the next 48 hours:**
1) Founder does task 1.
2) PM schedules the retro (task 2) for this week.
3) Founder announces task 4 in the team channel, in their own words.

### How we will know (metrics per quadrant)
- **I:** founder's self-rated urgency/anxiety (1–10) each Friday; engineers' sense of
  ownership in a 1-question pulse.
- **It:** % of sprint scope completed; number of mid-sprint changes.
- **We:** "I can disagree with the founder safely" (1–5) in a monthly anonymous pulse.
- **Its:** cycle time per task; WIP per engineer.
