# States and Types — Field Guide

## Part 1 — States

States are temporary. They come and go, while stages stay. Planning goes wrong when the
two are confused: a burned-out team (state) is not an incompetent team (stage), and a
great off-site high (state) is not a transformed culture (stage).

### The great natural states

| State | Body / energy | Typical experience | Contemplative name |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waking | Gross | The physical, sensory world | Gross |
| Dreaming | Subtle | Images, visions, flows of energy, insight, imagination | Subtle |
| Deep sleep | Causal | Formless, spacious, silent | Causal |
| Witnessing | — | Awareness that watches all states | Turiya / the Witness |
| Nondual | — | Awareness and its contents are one | Turiyatita / nondual |

Contemplative traditions describe a **state-stage** path: with practice, a person can
learn to stay aware through more subtle states, from gross to subtle to causal to
nondual. This is **Waking Up**, a different axis from **Growing Up** through
structure-stages.

### Everyday states that matter in analysis

- **Stress and threat** — narrows perception, increases reactivity, pulls people down
  to earlier levels of functioning.
- **Burnout and exhaustion** — lowers access to every line, especially emotional and
  interpersonal.
- **Flow** — high skill meets high challenge; peak performance.
- **Peak experiences** — sudden insight, awe, love, unity. Powerful, but they fade
  unless followed by practice and structure.
- **Group states** — panic, euphoria, collective grief, crowd excitement, a team "in
  the zone".
- **Chemical states** — caffeine, alcohol, sleep deprivation, medication, hunger.

### The Wilber-Combs lattice

Any state can be experienced from any stage, and is **interpreted through** that stage.

| Stage ↓ / State → | Gross | Subtle | Causal | Nondual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amber | Ordinary life seen through rules | Vision of a mythic deity | Awe of a transcendent God | Union with God in mythic terms |
| Orange | Ordinary life seen through data | "Creative insight", "a dream state" | "Deep relaxation" | "A neurological event of unity" |
| Green | Ordinary life seen through relationship | Felt connection with all life | Sacred silence of the Earth | Oneness with everyone |
| Teal | Ordinary life seen as an evolving system | Subtle energies as part of the system | Ground of the system | Awareness of the whole as itself |

**Practical use:** when someone reports a powerful experience, help them integrate it
at their current stage, without inflating it into a permanent stage.

### State questions

- What state are the key people mostly in? Is that normal for them or temporary?
- Which decisions are being made under stress, fear or euphoria?
- What practices could shift the state (rest, breathwork, a pause, a celebration)?
- What would change if everyone were well-rested?

## Part 2 — Types

Types are horizontal: styles that exist at every level. A type is not better or worse;
each has strengths and blind spots.

### Masculine and feminine (in every person)

| | Masculine principle | Feminine principle |
|---|---|---|
| Core drive | Agency, autonomy | Communion, relationship |
| Ethics | Rights, justice, rules | Care, responsibility, context |
| Healthy | Clarity, boundaries, decisiveness, freedom | Connection, empathy, flow, nurture |
| Unhealthy | Domination, alienation, coldness | Fusion, loss of self, enmeshment |

These are not about gender identity: every person and every culture has both, in
different proportions.

### Common typologies

- **Enneagram** — nine types organized around core motivations (1 Reformer,
  2 Helper, 3 Achiever, 4 Individualist, 5 Investigator, 6 Loyalist, 7 Enthusiast,
  8 Challenger, 9 Peacemaker). Each type can be lived at any level of development.
- **Big Five** — openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness,
  neuroticism (emotional stability). The most empirically supported model.
- **MBTI / Jungian types** — introversion/extraversion, sensing/intuition,
  thinking/feeling, judging/perceiving. Popular; treat as conversation aid.
- **Learning styles, communication styles, conflict styles** (e.g. Thomas-Kilmann).
- **Cultural dimensions** — individualism/collectivism, power distance, high/low
  context, attitudes to uncertainty and time.
- **Body types** — constitution, energy patterns, temperament.

Use whichever typology the user knows. Never force a type on someone; offer it as a
hypothesis.

### Type questions

- Which types are present, and which are missing from the room?
- Is the friction between two people a level difference or a type difference?
- Does the plan suit only one type (e.g. extroverted, action-oriented)?
- Which balance between agency and communion does the situation need right now?
