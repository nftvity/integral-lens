# Integral Practice — Growing the Whole Person (or Team)

Use for Grow mode: personal development, coaching, leadership development, team
development, or whenever the plan needs I-tasks that actually change people.

## The Four Ups

| Up | Axis | Question | Example practices |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wake Up** | States | Am I present and aware? | Meditation, mindfulness, breathwork, contemplative prayer, time in nature |
| **Grow Up** | Stages | Can I take bigger perspectives? | Perspective-taking, reading across worldviews, mentoring, developmental coaching, "immunity to change" maps |
| **Clean Up** | Shadow | What am I disowning or projecting? | 3-2-1 process, therapy, journaling, repair conversations, somatic work |
| **Show Up** | Embodiment & action | Do I live it in body, relationships, work and the world? | Exercise, keeping commitments, service, ethical action, craft |

Each Up is independent: someone can be highly awake (deep meditator), not very grown
up (ethnocentric views), full of shadow and barely showing up in their relationships.
An integral plan includes at least a little of each.

## Integral Life Practice (ILP)

ILP is a modular, cross-training approach to practice. Pick at least one practice in
each **core module**, then add **auxiliary modules** as needed.

### Core modules
- **Body** — strength, cardio, nutrition, sleep; also subtle-body practices (yoga,
  tai chi, qigong) and causal-body practices (deep relaxation).
- **Mind** — reading and study, perspective-taking, applying the AQAL map to your
  life, learning frameworks.
- **Spirit** — meditation, prayer, contemplation, gratitude, compassion practice.
- **Shadow** — 3-2-1 process, therapy, dream work, journaling, emotional
  transmutation.

### Auxiliary modules
- **Ethics** — keeping integrity, clarifying values, service.
- **Sex and intimacy** — conscious intimacy, healthy relationship with desire.
- **Work** — right livelihood, mastery, work as practice.
- **Emotions** — emotional awareness, regulation, transmutation.
- **Relationships** — communication, conflict skills, family, community.

### The 1-minute module idea
When time is short, do a one-minute version of each core module (one minute of
breathing, one minute of AQAL reflection, one minute of 3-2-1, one minute of
exercise). Consistency beats intensity.

## The 3-2-1 Shadow Process

For any person, trait or situation that triggers a strong reaction (attraction or
repulsion).

1. **3rd person — Face it.** Describe the person or quality in vivid detail, as
   "he / she / they / it". What exactly bothers or fascinates you?
2. **2nd person — Talk to it.** Speak directly to it, as "you". Ask: *What do you want
   from me? What are you trying to show me? Why are you here?* Let it answer.
3. **1st person — Be it.** Speak as it, in the first person: "I am …". Notice how this
   quality is also part of you. Feel it, own it, and integrate it.

Finish by asking: *what would change in my life if I owned this energy in a healthy
way?* Turn the answer into a Show Up task.

> Note for Claude: offer 3-2-1 as a reflective exercise. For trauma, severe distress or
> mental-health crises, recommend a qualified therapist and do not run it as therapy.

## Practices for Grow Up (stage development)

- **Perspective rotation.** Restate a disagreement from the other side's view so well
  that they would agree.
- **Subject-object shift.** Ask: what am I identified with (subject) that I could
  instead look at (object)? (Kegan.)
- **Immunity-to-change map** (Kegan & Lahey): goal → behaviors that work against it →
  hidden competing commitments → big assumptions → safe experiments to test them.
- **Polarity mapping:** treat tensions like stability/change or individual/team as
  polarities to manage, not problems to solve.
- **Developmental challenge + support.** Assignments slightly beyond the current level,
  with mentoring.

## Team version

| Up | Team practice |
|---|---|
| Wake Up | A minute of silence or a check-in at the start of meetings; sustainable pace |
| Grow Up | Rotating roles, cross-functional exposure, learning reviews |
| Clean Up | Blameless retrospectives, explicit conflict norms, repair rituals |
| Show Up | Keeping commitments, visible shared goals, service to customers and community |

## Psychograph to practice

From the psychograph (see [levels-and-lines.md](levels-and-lines.md)), take the
**limiting line** and pick one practice for it:

- Emotional → emotion labeling, journaling, somatic awareness
- Interpersonal → active listening, feedback practice, nonviolent communication
- Moral → values reflection, considering wider circles of impact
- Somatic → daily movement, sleep hygiene
- Cognitive → systems mapping, reading outside your field, steelmanning
- Spiritual → contemplative practice, meaning-making, service
