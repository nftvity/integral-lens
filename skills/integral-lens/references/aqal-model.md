# The AQAL Model — Core Reference

A working summary of Ken Wilber's Integral framework, written for practical use. It is an
independent interpretation, not an official text.

## 1. The intent of Integral Theory

Integral Theory tries to make one coherent map out of the partial truths of many fields
— the natural sciences, psychology, sociology, systems theory, contemplative
traditions, philosophy, the arts. Its starting assumption is that every serious
tradition is *true but partial*, so the question is not "who is right?" but "what does
each one see, and how do they fit together?"

The resulting map — AQAL — is meant to be the minimum set of dimensions you need to
check so that you do not leave anything essential out. Wilber calls it an *Integral
Operating System* (IOS): neutral software that can run any content, in any field.

## 2. Holons

A **holon** (a term from Arthur Koestler) is a whole that is also a part of a larger
whole: an atom inside a molecule inside a cell inside an organism inside an ecosystem.
Reality is made of holons, all the way up and all the way down.

Useful properties:

- **Agency and communion.** Every holon must keep its own wholeness (agency) *and* fit
  into the larger whole (communion). Too much agency → alienation; too much communion
  → loss of self.
- **Self-transcendence and self-dissolution.** Holons can grow into larger wholes or
  break down into their parts. Destroy a higher holon and the lower ones survive;
  destroy a lower one and every higher one built on it goes too (no cells, no organism).
- **Depth and span.** *Depth* is how many levels a holon contains; *span* is how many
  holons exist at a given level. Greater depth means less span (fewer organisms than
  cells).
- **Kinds of holon:**
  - *Individual holons* have a unified interior (a person, a dog, a cell).
  - *Social holons* have a shared, collective interior (a family, team, culture) but no
    single "I" in charge.
  - *Artifacts* (a hammer, an app, a building) are made by holons but have no interior
    of their own.
  - *Heaps* are mere collections (a pile of sand).

When you analyze anything, first ask which kind of holon it is. You cannot ask a
spreadsheet how it feels, but you can ask the team that uses it.

## 3. The four quadrants

Every holon can be seen from the **inside** or the **outside**, and as an
**individual** or a **collective**. That gives four irreducible perspectives.

| | Interior (felt, interpreted) | Exterior (observed, measured) |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | **Upper Left — "I"**. Intentional. Experience, thoughts, emotions, values, meaning, consciousness. Accessed by introspection and dialogue. | **Upper Right — "It"**. Behavioral. Body, brain, organism, behavior, skills. Accessed by observation and measurement. |
| **Collective** | **Lower Left — "We"**. Cultural. Shared meaning, worldviews, ethics, norms, language, relationships. Accessed by mutual understanding. | **Lower Right — "Its"**. Social/systemic. Structures, institutions, technology, economy, ecology, infrastructure. Accessed by systems analysis. |

### Validity claims

Each quadrant has its own way of being "right" (adapted from Jürgen Habermas):

- **UL — Truthfulness / sincerity.** Am I being honest about my experience?
- **UR — Truth / correspondence.** Does the statement match the observable facts?
- **LL — Justness / mutual fit.** Is this fair and right *for us*? Do we understand each other?
- **LR — Functional fit.** Does the system work? Does it fit into the larger web?

A claim that is valid in one quadrant cannot be checked using another quadrant's
methods. "My brain scan shows activity in the amygdala" (UR) does not tell you what the
fear *means* to me (UL).

### Tetra-arising

The quadrants are not separate realms. Every event has all four dimensions at once:
a thought (UL) has a brain state (UR), is expressed in a shared language (LL), and
occurs within a social system (LR). This is why lasting change almost always requires
intervention in all four.

### Quadrivia

The quadrants are *perspectives that every holon has*. The **quadrivia** are the
*perspectives you can take* on something: you can look at a rock from any quadrant's
point of view (how does it look? what does it mean to us?), even though the rock has no
interior of its own. In practice: map the holon's quadrants when it has them, and map
the stakeholders' quadrants when it does not.

## 4. The Big Three

The four quadrants are often simplified to three, because the two Right-Hand quadrants
are both "objective":

| Big Three | Quadrant | Value sphere | Classic name |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | UL | Art, self-expression, aesthetics | **The Beautiful** |
| **We** | LL | Morals, ethics, culture | **The Good** |
| **It / Its** | UR + LR | Science, technology, objective nature | **The True** |

Wilber's critique of modernity: it rightly *differentiated* the Big Three (science no
longer had to obey the church) but then *dissociated* them, and finally collapsed the
I and the We into the It — "flatland", where only what can be measured is considered
real. An integral approach re-includes all three.

## 5. Levels (stages, waves, altitudes)

Development unfolds in stages. Each stage **transcends and includes** the previous one:
it adds new capacities but keeps the healthy core of the earlier ones. Stages emerge
in sequence and cannot be skipped, although people can have *experiences* (states) of
higher realities at any stage.

A simple three-step version that applies almost everywhere:

1. **Egocentric** — me, my needs.
2. **Ethnocentric** — us, my group, my tribe, my nation.
3. **Worldcentric** — all of us, all people, regardless of race, religion, gender.
4. (**Kosmocentric** — all beings, all of reality.)

Integral Theory uses a **color spectrum** ("altitude") as a neutral ruler that can be
applied to any line. See [levels-and-lines.md](levels-and-lines.md) for the full table,
including how colors map to Spiral Dynamics, Kegan, Cook-Greuter and others.

Key ideas:

- **Center of gravity.** People and cultures operate across a range, but there is a
  typical level they return to.
- **Healthy vs. unhealthy versions.** Every level has a healthy and a pathological
  expression. Healthy Amber is loyalty and order; unhealthy Amber is rigid dogma.
- **First tier vs. second tier.** In first-tier stages (up to Green) each level tends to
  think its values are the only correct ones. Second tier (Teal onward) can value
  *every* level and its role in the whole.
- **Translation and transformation.** *Translation* is a horizontal move — making
  life work better at the current level. *Transformation* is a vertical move to a new
  level. Both are needed; most change efforts are translation.

## 6. Lines (streams, intelligences)

Development is not one line. People grow in many relatively independent
**developmental lines** — cognitive, emotional, interpersonal, moral, self-identity,
values, needs, aesthetic, kinesthetic, spiritual and more. Each line goes through the
same altitudes, but at different speeds.

- The **cognitive line** tends to lead: you can only act on what you can see, so
  cognitive development is necessary but not sufficient for development in the other
  lines.
- The **self line** (identity) is the "navigator" that tries to balance all the lines.
- A **psychograph** is a sketch of a person's or group's development across lines.
  Uneven development is normal; dangerous gaps (e.g. high cognitive, low moral) are worth
  naming.

## 7. States

States are **temporary** conditions of consciousness: they come and go, while stages,
once reached, are stable.

- **Natural great states:** waking (gross), dreaming (subtle), deep dreamless sleep
  (causal), plus the **Witness** (pure awareness of all states) and the **nondual**
  (awareness and its contents as one).
- **Altered and peak states:** flow, awe, meditation, intoxication, crisis, burnout,
  falling in love, panic.
- **The Wilber-Combs lattice:** any state can be experienced from any stage, and it
  will be *interpreted* through that stage. A mystical experience at Amber is told as a
  meeting with a mythic God; at Orange as a neurochemical event; at Green as
  connection with all life. Waking Up (states) and Growing Up (stages) are two
  different axes.

See [states-and-types.md](states-and-types.md).

## 8. Types

Types are **horizontal** differences — styles that show up at every level:

- masculine/feminine (agency, autonomy, rights, justice vs. communion, relationship,
  care, responsibility — present in every person to different degrees)
- personality typologies (Enneagram, Big Five, MBTI)
- learning, communication and cultural styles
- body types

Many conflicts blamed on "levels" are really about types, and vice versa.

## 9. Core principles

- **Nonexclusion.** Accept the valid truths of every approach within its own domain.
  Different methods bring forth different, real phenomena.
- **Enfoldment (unfoldment).** Some truths are more inclusive than others; a more
  inclusive view should embrace the partial view rather than dismiss it.
- **Enactment.** Phenomena are brought forth by the practices (injunctions) used to see
  them. If you want to know whether a meditation works, you have to meditate; if you
  want to see the moons of Jupiter, you need a telescope.
- **The three strands of valid knowledge:** (1) *injunction* — if you want to know
  this, do this; (2) *apprehension* — see what arises; (3) *confirmation or rejection*
  — check it with others who have done the injunction.
- **Integral Methodological Pluralism.** Each quadrant can be studied from its own inside
  and outside, giving **eight zones** of methodology. See [eight-zones.md](eight-zones.md).
- **The pre/trans fallacy.** Pre-rational and trans-rational states are both
  non-rational, so they are easily confused. See [pitfalls.md](pitfalls.md).

## 10. Key vocabulary

- **AQAL** — All Quadrants, All Levels, All Lines, All States, All Types.
- **Altitude** — a color-coded level of development, usable on any line.
- **Center of gravity** — the typical level from which someone acts.
- **Flatland** — the belief that only the exterior, measurable world (the Right-Hand
  quadrants) is real.
- **Holarchy** — a hierarchy of holons, where each higher level includes the lower.
- **Integral Methodological Pluralism (IMP)** — the use of all eight zones of methods.
- **Kosmos** — the whole of reality including its interior dimensions (as opposed to
  "cosmos", the physical universe only).
- **Prime directive** — support the health of the entire developmental spiral, not
  just one level.
- **Psychograph** — a profile of development across lines.
- **Shadow** — disowned or repressed parts of the self, which are then projected onto
  others.
- **Tetra-mesh** — a fit or balance across all four quadrants.
- **Transcend and include** — each higher level adds to, rather than discarding, the
  lower ones.
- **Vision-logic** — the cognitive capacity to hold many perspectives and systems at
  once, the cognitive basis of integral stages.
